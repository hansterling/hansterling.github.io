About Hannah

Hi! I’m Hannah, a passionate music educator who believes that learning music should feel encouraging, creative, and deeply human.

I work with students of all ages, helping them build strong musical foundations while also developing confidence, curiosity, and joy in music-making.

Whether you’re brand new to music or returning after time away, my goal is to meet you where you are and support your growth every step of the way.
